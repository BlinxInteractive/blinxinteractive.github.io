Pawventure

A cosy roguelite adventure with Rui the red panda, by Blinx Interactive.

Walk Rui through bamboo forests, misty mountains, lantern-lit villages and beyond. Every adventure is different: pick new skills as you level up, combine them into powerful evolutions, meet friendly pets, and take on chapter bosses.

Features

  • Eight chapters on three difficulties, each with its own monsters and boss
  • Over 60 skills and evolutions to build a new Rui every run
  • Pets, gear sets, talents and a home base to grow between adventures
  • The Endless Tower, weekly challenges, daily dungeons and seasonal events

Signing in

Pawventure doesn't need an account. On Android you can sign in with Google Play Games, which the game uses only to back up your progress to your own Google account and to show your achievements and leaderboards. See the privacy policy for details.
