Pawventure Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Pawventure is a game about Rui the red panda, made by Blinx Interactive ("we"). This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses handle, and the choices you have. In short: there are no accounts, we never ask for your name, email or location, and ads only play when you choose to watch one.

Your game progress

Your progress is saved on your device. If you turn on cloud save, a copy is also stored in your own iCloud account (iPhone and iPad) or in a hidden Pawventure folder in your own Google Drive (Android). Only the game can read that folder, and we can't see it. Transfer codes you create contain only your game progress.

Ads

In the mobile app you can choose to watch a short video ad for a bonus (marked with 📺). Ads never play unless you tap one of those buttons. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the ads you see and tap, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone and iPad, personalised ads are only shown if you allow tracking when asked. Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Crash reports

If the mobile app crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report to Sentry so we can find and fix the problem. The report contains the error, the app version, your phone's model and system version, and the screen you were on. It doesn't include your name, email, advertising ID or anything you type. Sentry's policy: sentry.io/privacy.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your phone itself; no server is involved. Turn them off any time in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check purchases, which records the items bought in the app, together with a random ID (not your name or email), so the Supporter Pack can be restored on a new device. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

Pawventure is made for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes the progress on your device. You can delete the cloud copy from your iCloud or Google Drive settings, or use Settings → Reset all progress in the game (with cloud save on, the reset save replaces the cloud copy). To reset your advertising ID, use your device's privacy settings.
